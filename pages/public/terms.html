TERMS OF USE

Use Urban Living PG responsibly.

Product-stage terms covering accounts, listings, bookings, payments, acceptable use and the limits of the UI prototype.

Accounts

Users should provide accurate information, protect credentials and use their own account. Production role permissions are controlled by the backend.

Listings and bookings

Availability, rent, deposits, fees, room/bed assignment and booking status must be confirmed by the production service.

Payments

Client-side UI must never be the authority for successful payment. Server-side provider verification and reconciliation are required.

Acceptable use

No fraud, impersonation, unlawful listings, unauthorized access, harassment, malicious code or abuse of payment/maintenance systems.
