HOW IT WORKS

One living journey from search to move-out

Urban Living PG combines apartment rental and PG/managed-stay workflows without forcing tenants to learn different products.

1

Discover

Choose apartment, private PG, shared PG or co-living and filter precisely.

2

Decide

Compare rent, deposit, occupancy, meals, amenities, rules and availability.

3

Reserve

Request a visit or continue to reservation and required verification.

4

Move in

Complete agreement, payment and move-in checklist.

5

Live

Manage rent, receipts, documents, maintenance and support.

6

Move out

Submit notice, complete inspection, settle dues and track deposit settlement.
